🌊 Avcılar — Denizköşkler Coastal Fill
Soil Amplification Factor: ~2.4x Resonance

In the 1999 Gölcük earthquake, Avcılar was 110 km away from the fault rupture, yet experienced catastrophic building collapses.

The Cause: Loose silty-clay marine sediments (Avcılar Formation) trapped seismic shear waves, amplifying ground oscillation duration by 240%.

AfetGuard Intervention: Triggers mandatory steel L-profile bracing on all tall furniture to prevent structural resonance whips.

🌧️ Kağıthane — Cendere Historical Stream Bed
Water Table Saturation: Rapid Backflow Risk

The Cendere Valley was historically an active tributary draining into the Golden Horn. In sudden torrential rain, ground absorption drops to zero within 15 minutes.

The Cause: Stormwater sewers backflow into ground-floor and basement apartments before residents can react.

AfetGuard Intervention: Mandates breaker shutoff before water contact and vertical evacuation to higher floors.

Ethical Guardrails & Trust (15 Points)

Strict Legal, Structural, and Medical Safety Boundaries

09 / 10
Ethical Risk Mitigation
Preventing AI Hallucinations in Life-or-Death Contexts
1. No Structural Engineering Guarantees

The Risk: An AI model declaring "your building is 100% earthquake-proof" or "this building will definitely collapse."

Our Guardrail: The model is system-prompted to explicitly disclaim structural assessments. It mandates that official core sampling (karot) and soil tests must be performed by certified engineering chambers.

2. No Medical Prescriptions or Dosages

The Risk: An AI prescribing medication substitutes or altered insulin doses during disaster shortages.

Our Guardrail: The model is strictly banned from recommending drug names or dosages. It only plans physical logistics: thermal ice packs for existing physician-prescribed insulin and backup prescription copies.

3. Official 112 Emergency Protocol Only

The Risk: Directing users to unauthorized civilian volunteer channels or unmonitored frequencies.

Our Guardrail: Adheres strictly to official Ministry of Interior 112 Emergency Call Center guidelines and AFAD regional radio coordination.

4. Strict Client-Side Privacy

The Risk: Storing private bedroom photos or home addresses on remote database servers.

Our Guardrail: Room images are converted to Base64 in memory, transmitted securely via HTTPS to Gemini, and never written to server disk. API keys reside in browser localStorage.

Fully Compliant with UNDRR, AFAD & Turkish Red Crescent Protocols
